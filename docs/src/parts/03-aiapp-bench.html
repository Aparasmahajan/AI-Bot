5.2Leave behind

  • The Electron shell. A Chromium-based app needs 150 MB or more before it does any work; Wispr Flow shows where that ends up. MWB's always-on part is a small Python tray process, and windows open only when you ask for them.
  • The overlay features: the always-on-top window excluded from screen capture, the custom pointer, OCR screen-ask and auto-answer. MWB is a visible assistant, and a meeting recorder has to show that it is recording.
  • Any borrowed identity in packaging. MWB ships under its own name and icon. On a managed laptop, an audio-capturing process that presents itself as a different product is exactly what endpoint security flags.

6.4What the numbers decide

  1. Dictation uses 4 threads. You are waiting for the text, and 4 threads cut a 3-second phrase from 1.66 s to 0.66 s.
  2. Meetings use 2 threads at below-normal priority. The call app, whichever it is, needs the CPU during a call. Rev G: meetings now use whisper small, not Parakeet (decision 5). On 2 threads it keeps up only on mains power (1.88× real time), so on battery the transcript is made after the call (§6.5).
  3. Never send Parakeet more than about 10 seconds of audio at once. Cutting the 62-second file into 10-second pieces was faster (10.8 s against 12.5 s) and used 530 MB less. whisper is the opposite: it always works on a 30-second window, so meeting pieces are 22–28 s (§6.5).
  4. Background meeting work runs with the onnxruntime arena off. Memory stays flat at 0.99 GB, at a cost of about 20 % more time. Dictation keeps the arena on for speed, and the process is recycled after 15 idle minutes.
  5. Start loading the model when the hotkey goes down. Loading takes 7.5 to 12 s and a typical phrase lasts 3 to 8 s, so most of the wait hides behind your speech.
  6. Keep whisper.cpp for meetings, and Parakeet for dictation. On English, Parakeet was 1.7× faster and made no recognition errors where whisper base.en made two, so dictation stays on Parakeet. The multilingual small model, measured on 12 October, is slower on English (2.6× real time, 4.2 % WER), but it understands Hindi and Hinglish, which your meetings use (decision 5, 13 October). So every meeting goes through whisper small, in the background. Large-v3-turbo, downloaded on 12 October, is slower than real time here (0.45×), so on this laptop it is only for overnight re-runs.
  7. Keep numpy and onnxruntime out of the always-on process. Python starts at 16 MB. Importing those two libraries costs about 250 MB before any model loads.
