## Decisions taken from these numbers

1. Dictation: 4 threads, arena on, recycle the worker after 15 idle minutes.
2. Meetings: 2 threads, below-normal priority, arena off (flat ~0.99 GB).
3. Never send more than ~10 s of audio per request.
4. Start loading the model on hotkey-down (load 7.5–12 s overlaps speech).
5. whisper.cpp only for Hindi / Hinglish.
6. Keep numpy / onnxruntime out of the always-on core process.
7. whisper small (multilingual) only for Hindi / Hinglish, in the background (12 Oct).
8. whisper large-v3-turbo is too slow on this CPU for everyday use (0.45× real time). Use it on a laptop with a GPU, or for overnight re-runs of Hindi meetings (12 Oct).
9. Local fallback: Qwen3.5 2B every day (fits next to the speech worker), 4B for code and the journal when there is room; thinking off; long prompts on Vulkan (12 Oct).

## Reproduce (PowerShell)

```powershell
$py = "$env:USERPROFILE\Documents\New folder\AI-App\addon\parakeet\.venv\Scripts\python.exe"
cd "$env:USERPROFILE\Documents\New folder\MWB\docs\bench"
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File make_wav.ps1 -Out "$PWD\standup.wav" -TextFile "$PWD\ref.txt"
& $py bench_parakeet.py standup.wav ref.txt 4
& $py bench_parakeet_mem.py standup.wav 4 1      # arena on
& $py bench_parakeet_mem.py standup.wav 4 0      # arena off
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File bench_whisper.ps1 -Wav "$PWD\standup.wav" -Threads 4
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File bench_whisper.ps1 -Wav "$PWD\standup.wav" -Threads 4 -Model "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\MWB\models\ggml-small.bin"   # whisper small, 12 Oct
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File bench_whisper.ps1 -Wav "$PWD\standup.wav" -Threads 4 -Model "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\MWB\models\ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0.bin"   # turbo, 12 Oct
& "$env:USERPROFILE\Tools\llamacpp\cpu\llama-bench.exe" -m $env:USERPROFILE\Tools\llamacpp\models\Qwen3.5-2B-Q4_K_M.gguf -t 4 -p 512 -n 128 -r 2   # also the 4B; the vulkan build with -ngl 99
```
